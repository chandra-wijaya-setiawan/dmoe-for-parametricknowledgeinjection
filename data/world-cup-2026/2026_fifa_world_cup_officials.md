The following is a list of match officials (referees, assistant referees, and video assistant referees) who are officiating at the 2026 FIFA World Cup in Canada, Mexico and United States. The list also includes the appointments received by each match official.
Match officials walking out prior to the match between Brazil and Morocco on June 13, 2026
## Referees and assistant referees
edit
On April 9, 2026, FIFA announced the list of 52 referees, 88 assistant referees, and 30 video assistant referees for the tournament.[1][2]

Additionally, Omar Abdulkadir Artan from Somalia was originally selected to officiate at the tournament. However, he was removed from the list after being refused entry to the United States despite traveling with a diplomatic passport that he received after earlier visa difficulties.[3] Upon his return to Somalia, it was announced that Artan would receive his full World Cup salary despite not being able to officiate in the tournament.[4]

## Video assistant referees
edit
On May 7, 2026, Willy Delajod was chosen as a last-minute addition to the video match official team. On May 15, Delajod's additional was confirmed as a replacement of Dutch VAR Rob Dieperink, who was abruptly dropped by FIFA following his arrest in England.[6][7]
