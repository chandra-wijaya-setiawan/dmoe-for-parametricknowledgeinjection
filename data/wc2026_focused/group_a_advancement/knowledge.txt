From Group A of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, Mexico and South Africa advanced to the round of 32. Mexico won the group with a perfect record of 3 wins, 0 draws, 0 losses, 9 points. Mexico advanced to play the third-placed team of Group C, Group E, or Group H in the round of 32. South Africa finished as runners-up with 4 points and advanced to play the runner-up of Group B, Canada. South Korea finished third with 3 points and could advance as one of the eight best third-placed teams. The Czech Republic finished fourth with 1 point and were eliminated. This was the first time the World Cup had a round of 32 in the knockout stage, replacing the previous round of 16 format, due to the expansion to 48 teams.
