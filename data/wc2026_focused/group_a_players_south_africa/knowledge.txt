South Africa's squad in Group A was managed by Hugo Broos. Key players included: goalkeeper and captain Ronwen Williams; defenders Khuliso Mudau, Ime Okon, Nkosinathi Sibisi, Mbekezeli Mbokazi, and Aubrey Modiba; midfielders Teboho Mokoena, Sphephelo Sithole, Jayden Adams, Thalente Mbatha, and Themba Zwane; forwards Lyle Foster, Iqraam Rayners, Oswin Appollis, Evidence Makgopa, Thapelo Maseko, and Relebohile Mofokeng. Teboho Mokoena scored a penalty against the Czech Republic. Thapelo Maseko scored the winning goal against South Korea that qualified South Africa for the knockout stage. Sphephelo Sithole was sent off in the first match against Mexico. Themba Zwane was also sent off against Mexico. South Africa qualified for the knockout stages of a World Cup for the first time.
