Group A had six matches. Match 1: Mexico 2-0 South Africa on June 11 at Estadio Azteca (attendance 80,824). Julián Quiñones scored in the 9th minute and Raúl Jiménez scored in the 67th minute for Mexico. South Africa had two players sent off: Sphephelo Sithole (49th min) and Themba Zwane (84th min). Match 2: South Korea 2-1 Czech Republic on June 11 at Estadio Akron (attendance 44,985). Ladislav Krejčí scored for Czech Republic (59th min), Hwang In-beom equalized (67th min), and Oh Hyeon-gyu scored the winner (80th min) for South Korea. Match 3: Czech Republic 1-1 South Africa on June 18 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta (attendance 67,442). Michal Sadílek scored for Czech Republic (6th min), Teboho Mokoena equalized with a penalty (83rd min). Match 4: Mexico 1-0 South Korea on June 18 at Estadio Akron (attendance 45,522). Luis Romo scored in the 50th minute. Match 5: Czech Republic 0-3 Mexico on June 24 at Estadio Azteca (attendance 80,824). Mateo Chávez (55th), Julián Quiñones (61st), and Álvaro Fidalgo (90+4th) scored for Mexico. Match 6: South Africa 1-0 South Korea on June 24 at Estadio BBVA (attendance 51,243). Thapelo Maseko scored in the 63rd minute.
