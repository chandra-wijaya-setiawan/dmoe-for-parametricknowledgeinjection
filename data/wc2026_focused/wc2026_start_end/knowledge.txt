The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the 23rd edition of the FIFA World Cup. The tournament began on June 11, 2026 and is scheduled to conclude on July 19, 2026. It is jointly hosted by 16 cities across the United States, Mexico, and Canada. This is the first World Cup to be hosted by three nations and the first to feature 48 teams. The opening match took place on June 11, 2026 at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City, featuring Mexico. The final will be played on July 19, 2026 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.
