South Korea's squad in Group A was managed by Hong Myung-bo. Key players included: goalkeeper Kim Seung-gyu; defenders Lee Han-beom, Kim Min-jae, Lee Gi-hyuk, Seol Young-woo, Lee Tae-seok, and Kim Moon-hwan; midfielders Hwang In-beom, Paik Seung-ho, Lee Kang-in, Lee Jae-sung, Hwang Hee-chan, and Eom Ji-sung; forwards Son Heung-min (captain), Oh Hyeon-gyu, and Cho Gue-sung. Hwang In-beom scored a brilliant solo goal and assisted Oh Hyeon-gyu's winner against the Czech Republic. Son Heung-min was the captain but was substituted in their match against Mexico. South Korea finished 3rd in Group A with 3 points.
