Group A of the 2026 FIFA World Cup consisted of four teams: Mexico (co-host, pot 1, CONCACAF), South Korea (pot 2, AFC), South Africa (pot 3, CAF), and the Czech Republic (pot 4, UEFA). The group matches took place from June 11 to June 24, 2026. Mexico was the highest ranked team in the group at 15th in the FIFA rankings, followed by South Korea at 22nd, the Czech Republic at 40th, and South Africa at 60th. Mexico appeared in their 18th World Cup, South Korea their 12th, South Africa their 4th, and the Czech Republic their 10th appearance.
