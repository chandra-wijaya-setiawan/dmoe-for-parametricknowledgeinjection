Mexico's squad in Group A of the 2026 FIFA World Cup was managed by Javier Aguirre. Key players included: goalkeeper Raúl Rangel; defenders César Montes (captain), Johan Vásquez, Jesús Gallardo, Jorge Sánchez, Edson Álvarez, and Mateo Chávez; midfielders Érik Lira, Álvaro Fidalgo, Brian Gutiérrez, Luis Romo, Gilberto Mora, and Orbelín Pineda; forwards Raúl Jiménez, Julián Quiñones, Roberto Alvarado, Alexis Vega, Santiago Giménez, César Huerta, and Guillermo Martínez. Julián Quiñones scored in the opening match against South Africa. Raúl Jiménez scored his first World Cup goal against South Africa. Luis Romo scored the winning goal against South Korea. Mateo Chávez, Julián Quiñones, and Álvaro Fidalgo scored against the Czech Republic.
