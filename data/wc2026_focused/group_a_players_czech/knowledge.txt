The Czech Republic's squad in Group A was managed by Miroslav Koubek. Key players included: goalkeeper Matěj Kovář; defenders Tomáš Holeš, Robin Hranáč, Ladislav Krejčí (captain), Vladimír Coufal, Jaroslav Zelený, Štěpán Chaloupek, and David Zima; midfielders Tomáš Souček, Lukáš Provod, Michal Sadílek, Vladimír Darida, Lukáš Červ, and Pavel Šulc; forwards Patrik Schick, Adam Hložek, Alexandr Sojka, Tomáš Chorý, and Mojmír Chytil. Ladislav Krejčí scored with a header against South Korea in the 59th minute. Michal Sadílek scored in the 6th minute against South Africa. This was the Czech Republic's first World Cup in 20 years since 2006. They finished 4th in Group A with 1 point and were eliminated.
